# Soft Signal Guard

Status: `REQUIRED_DEFAULT_PROTOCOL`

1. `MECHANICAL_TOPOLOGY != SOFT_SIGNAL`. Decade direction/order, fixed palace-role landing and derived positional coincidence are retrieval only.
2. One physical/source identity may support a signal once. Borrowed/opposite/relation views do not create new paths.
3. `NO_DOMAIN_REASSIGNMENT` remains binding. A residence fact cannot rescue a Career signal; native-parent support cannot rescue a Marriage-resource signal.
4. Core always has priority. A conflicting Soft Signal is suppressed or marked `CONFLICT_DISCLOSED`; it cannot alter Core.
5. Soft Signals cannot upgrade `UNRESOLVED` to a formal Core category. In particular, `SPOUSE_SIDE_RESOURCE = UNRESOLVED` may coexist with `SPOUSE_SIDE_RESOURCE_SIGNAL = HAS_SIGNAL`, but not with `RESOURCE_STRONG` unless the Core Gate independently passes.
6. Forbidden language: `一定`, `必然`, `注定`, `百分百`, `一定发财`, `一定离婚`, `一定创业`, `一定富二代`, `一定进体制`.
7. Preferred language names tendency and condition: “特征比较明显”, “有一点这个象，可以留意”, “更像…”, “如果现实里出现合适载体，这个方向比较容易被放大”, or “只能算辅助信号，不建议单独据此下结论”.
8. More than five proactive signals is `FAIL`; a single auxiliary-only item is hidden by default.
