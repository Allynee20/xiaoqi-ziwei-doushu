# Decade Resource Band

Status: `ACCEPTED_PROJECT_SCHEMA`

Every decade is positioned relative to the subject's frozen `NATAL_RESOURCE_BASELINE`:

- `BELOW_BASELINE`: core earning vehicle is pressured/interrupted, retention collapses, or a resource channel is lost.
- `AT_BASELINE`: natal capacity is broadly expressed without clear structural upgrade or contraction.
- `ABOVE_BASELINE`: stronger vehicle/control, added resource channel, or better retention clearly activates.
- `BREAKOUT_ABOVE_BASELINE`: a structural upgrade such as Position→Business/Platform, L2→L3/L4, single income→multi-resource network, labor vehicle→replicable operating vehicle, or a strong spouse/family platform truly enters the Career–Wealth System.

`BREAKOUT_ABOVE_BASELINE` is not a net-worth guarantee and never licenses exact amounts.

## Required output

For each decade state: band, non-mechanical evidence, comparison to the natal baseline, limiting factor, and miss condition. A decade may have high scale and poor retention; band reasoning must name both.
