# Mechanical Topology Guard

Status: `REQUIRED_DEFAULT_PROTOCOL`

## Hard rule

`MECHANICAL_TOPOLOGY != INDIVIDUAL_SIGNAL`.

Forward/reverse decade direction, ordinal decade number, and palace positions created solely by the fixed twelve-palace topology are retrieval paths. They cannot be counted as personalized evidence.

Example: “decade Wealth role falls on natal Spouse palace” does not by itself support marriage wealth, wealthy spouse, or wealthy spouse family.

## Required continuation

After topology selects a physical palace, the Reasoner must inspect:

1. physical palace contents and preserved source identity;
2. decade-stem transforms and their physical source stars;
3. legal Sanfang/Sizheng relations without vote duplication;
4. explicit resource pathway;
5. retention/asset pathway;
6. delta versus the previous decade;
7. band versus the Natal Resource Baseline.

If no non-mechanical evidence remains, output `UNRESOLVED`.

## Audit record

Every decade output must list `MECHANICAL_ONLY_FACTS` separately from `INDIVIDUAL_EVIDENCE_USED`. Mechanical-only facts may navigate retrieval but must not appear in the evidence count, baseline classification, regime, band, or event conclusion.
