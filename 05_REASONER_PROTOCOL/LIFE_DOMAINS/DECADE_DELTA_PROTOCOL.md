# Decade Delta Protocol

Status: `REQUIRED_DEFAULT_PROTOCOL`

Allowed regimes: `BUILD`, `EXPAND`, `CONSOLIDATE`, `RESTRUCTURE`, `HIGH_SCALE_HIGH_RISK`, `CONTRACT`.

For every decade answer:

1. what resource is added versus the prior decade;
2. what resource is lost or pressured;
3. Career Vehicle upgrade/downgrade;
4. Control increase/decrease;
5. Scalability increase/decrease;
6. Retention increase/decrease;
7. Band versus Natal Baseline;
8. marriage-resource delta;
9. principal tradeoff;
10. mechanical-only topology facts excluded from evidence.

Delta is comparative, not a score. The same physical evidence across Natal/Decade layers is resonance/reactivation, not another vote.

## Lifecycle Template Guard

Never auto-generate “30s begin growth / 40s get rich / 50s preserve wealth.” If two charts share an age sequence, their physical evidence paths must independently justify it. Otherwise label the lifecycle sentence `NON_DISCRIMINATIVE` and remove it.

The standard decade comparison horizon is `CURRENT_PLUS_NEXT_FOUR`; the immediately previous decade is loaded only as the comparison baseline for the current decade.
