# Career–Wealth System

Status: `ACCEPTED_PROJECT_SCHEMA`

`CAREER_WEALTH_SYSTEM` unifies the analysis route while keeping these dimensions independent:

- `CAREER_VEHICLE`
- `CONTROL_LEVEL`
- `RESOURCE_SOURCE`
- `SCALABILITY`
- `RETENTION`
- `NATAL_RESOURCE_BASELINE`
- `DECADE_RESOURCE_BAND`
- `DECADE_DELTA`

## Career Vehicle

Allowed values: `POSITION_BASED`, `PROFESSIONAL_SKILL`, `PROJECT_BASED`, `ORGANIZATION_PLATFORM`, `BUSINESS_OWNERSHIP`, `UNRESOLVED`.

Entrepreneurial experience does not prove mature `BUSINESS_OWNERSHIP`; owning a registered company does not establish a high career level.

## Control Level

- `L1_EXECUTION`: completes tasks defined by others.
- `L2_RESULT_OWNERSHIP`: owns a complete result.
- `L3_RESOURCE_CONTROL`: can mobilize people, money, clients, budget, or organizational resources.
- `L4_SCALABLE_PLATFORM`: the vehicle can replicate and expand without relying only on the subject's direct labor.

Job title never determines Control Level. A level must be supported by observable responsibility and resource paths.

## Resource Source

Analyze separately: `SELF_EARNED`, `CAREER_DERIVED`, `BUSINESS_OWNERSHIP`, `FAMILY_RESOURCE`, `SPOUSE_SIDE_RESOURCE`, `PROPERTY_ASSET`. A present source is not automatically dominant.

## Wealth chain

```text
NATAL_BASELINE
→ SOURCE
→ SCALABILITY
→ RETENTION
→ DECADE_RESOURCE_BAND
→ DECADE_DELTA
```

`HIGH_SCALE != HIGH_RETENTION`. Earning, holding, family wealth, subject ownership, property, and decade activation remain separate.

## Prohibitions

No star equals wealth; no good palace equals rich family; house ownership does not establish wealth; family wealth does not establish subject wealth; earning does not establish retention; a high natal baseline does not make every decade wealthy; a favorable decade does not mean ten uninterrupted years of profit.
