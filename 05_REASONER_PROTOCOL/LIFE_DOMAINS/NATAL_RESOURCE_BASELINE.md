# Natal Resource Baseline

Status: `ACCEPTED_PROJECT_SCHEMA`

The baseline is a long-run resource-carrying interval before any one decade's amplification or contraction. It is not a precise asset prediction.

## Allowed classes

- `ORDINARY_RESOURCE_STRUCTURE` (display: 普通): primarily one labor/career income route; weak scalable vehicle; limited established family/spouse resource; ordinary retention; continuing personal labor remains important.
- `AFFLUENT_RESOURCE_STRUCTURE` (display: 小富 / 中等偏上): at least two independent directions such as strong career/operating ability, asset base, multiple resource entrances, family/spouse-side resources, retention, or a moderately scalable vehicle.
- `HIGH_WEALTH_CAPACITY_STRUCTURE` (display: 高资源 / 大富候选): multiple independent structures jointly support scalability, resource density, L3/L4 control candidate, more than one channel, and retention/asset capacity, or an exceptionally strong family/spouse/business platform. Must state `HIGH_WEALTH_CAPACITY != GUARANTEED_HIGH_NET_WORTH`.
- `UNRESOLVED`: insufficient or conflicting evidence.

## Mandatory structural inspection

1. self earning formation;
2. Career Vehicle and natural Control Level;
3. Scalability;
4. Retention / Asset Base;
5. Family Resource;
6. Spouse-side Resource;
7. Resource Channel Diversity.

No score, auspicious/malefic vote, or duplicate physical evidence is allowed. A single star, transform, or palace cannot establish `AFFLUENT` or `HIGH_WEALTH_CAPACITY`. Exact wealth numbers are prohibited.

The output must include the class, independent supporting paths, limiting paths, same-evidence deduplication, and a `MISS_CONDITION`.
