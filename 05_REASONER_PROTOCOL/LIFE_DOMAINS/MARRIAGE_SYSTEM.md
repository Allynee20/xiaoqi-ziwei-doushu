## Strong Spouse Resource Gate

At least two independent directions must align:

- A: spouse-palace resource/platform property;
- B: spouse-derived resource/property structure;
- C: marriage channel independently connects into the native Career–Wealth System.

Only `A+B`, `A+C`, `B+C`, or all three with distinct physical evidence may support `RESOURCE_STRONG_CANDIDATE`. The same spouse-palace evidence seen again through a Career borrow/opposite view is one identity, not A+C.
