# Family Support L1–L5 Project Schema

Status: `ACCEPTED_PROJECT_SCHEMA`  
Origin label: `PROJECT_INVENTED_SCHEMA`

| Level | ID | Meaning | Minimum evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | `PRACTICAL_EMOTIONAL_SUPPORT` | Emotional/practical care, relationship support and family interaction | Parent/family-context evidence; says nothing about wealth |
| L2 | `EDUCATIONAL_INSTITUTIONAL_SUPPORT` | Education, documentation, institutional protection or guidance | Direct education/institution evidence; says nothing about family wealth |
| L3 | `RESOURCE_ACCESS` | Access to contacts, facilities, opportunities or family-controlled resources | Explicit access/channel evidence; access is not transfer |
| L4 | `FAMILY_WEALTH` | Family-level asset/wealth base | Direct family-asset evidence; subject need not receive it |
| L5 | `ACTUAL_WEALTH_TRANSFER_SOURCE` | Actual inheritance, transfer or wealth reaching the subject | Requires explicit transfer/source evidence; no natal-only automatic route is accepted |

Frozen non-equivalences:

```text
L1 != L4
L2 != L4
L3 != L5
L4 != L5
```

There is no automatic upgrade path between levels. A Property-palace ancestral branch may support only `family asset base / inheritance channel candidate`; it cannot establish that the subject's wealth comes from family.

## Parent object policy

Canonical rule: `NO_SAFE_GENERAL_FATHER_MOTHER_SPLIT`.

Default object: `PARENT_PAIR_CONTEXT`.

`太阳 = traditional paternal-associated signal` and `太阴 = traditional maternal-associated signal` may be retained only as `DESCRIPTIVE_ONLY` and `NOT_OBJECT_ASSIGNMENT`. They do not allocate all evidence to a parent, and they cannot support parent health, lifespan, or order-of-death claims.
