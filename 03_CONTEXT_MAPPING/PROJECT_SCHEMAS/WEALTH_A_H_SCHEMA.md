# Wealth A–H Project Schema

Status: `ACCEPTED_PROJECT_SCHEMA`  
Origin label: `PROJECT_INVENTED_SCHEMA`

This is a Reasoner-facing decomposition, not a traditional taxonomy.

| ID | Dimension | Question | Evidence interfaces | Hard boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| W-A | `EARNING_FORMATION` | How is earning formed or delivered? | Wealth, Career, external/client delivery paths | Formation != capacity != result |
| W-B | `EARNING_CAPACITY` | What resource/ability can be mobilized? | Direct ability, wealth handling, responsibility and support evidence | Opportunity != ability; capacity != actual income |
| W-C | `INCOME_STABILITY_VOLATILITY` | How stable or variable is the income pattern? | Income rhythm plus work/client continuity | Volatility != loss |
| W-D | `CASH_FLOW_VELOCITY` | How quickly does money/resources circulate? | Turnover, settlement, transaction and mobility evidence | Fast flow != loss or low retention |
| W-E | `RETENTION` | Can resources be preserved or sedimented? | Multiple interfaces: wealth handling, property/storage, fortune/consumption, career continuity, explicit cost evidence | **Retention belongs to no single palace** |
| W-F | `EROSION_OUTFLOW` | What mechanism creates cost, leakage or outflow? | Directional cost/friction/contract/consumption evidence | Ji, volatility or fast flow alone != loss |
| W-G | `PROPERTY_ASSET_BASE` | What fixed base, residence, operating base, property or family asset channel exists? | Property/Home plus explicit source branch | Strong Property != house ownership or wealth |
| W-H | `LONG_TERM_ACCUMULATION` | Do formation, retention and asset-base evidence align over time? | W-A through W-G with time scope | No single palace/star/transform can establish it |

## Canonical prohibitions

```text
strong Wealth palace != rich
strong Property palace != retention
good Property palace != owns a house
Ji != financial loss
volatility != loss
fast cash flow != loss
```

## Profile/source-bound local assets

- `祖业 / 自置 / 退祖 / 先破后有` are preserved as distinct property-source branches. They are not universal outcomes and retain source/profile status.
- Under the cited Zhongzhou material, `禄存` may support accumulation while `天马` may support circulation. This is `PROFILE_SPECIFIC`; neither implies amount, profit or loss.
- A Property-palace route can support `family asset base / inheritance channel candidate`; it cannot automatically support “the subject's wealth comes from family.”
- Ji may support friction, obstruction, competition or a star-specific cost mechanism. It cannot be upgraded directly to “loss” or “bankruptcy.”

All evidence keeps its physical palace, source identity, profile and uncertainty. No scoring or palace ownership of a dimension is authorized.
