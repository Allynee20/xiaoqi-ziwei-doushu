# Empty Palace and Borrow Grammar

Status: `ACCEPTED_CORE`

## Trigger

An empty palace is `MAIN_TONE_PENDING`, not “no information,” weak, bad, inactive or eventless. Borrow is considered only when the target's local main tone is empty/pending.

## Borrow object

Borrow the complete physical-opposite **main-tone group** (the opposite palace's complete main-star group). Do not relocate auxiliary, malefic or miscellaneous stars. They may participate only through their own legal LOCAL/OPPOSITE/SANHE roles.

## Source identity

- `Borrow != relocation`.
- Every borrowed main star keeps its physical/source palace and source evidence identity.
- An existing natal transform remains bound to its original source star; borrow creates no target-local transform.
- Raw brightness/state remains bound to its source star and source table/provider; borrow creates no brightness.
- A borrowed view cannot become the source of another projection.

## Target-local facts

The target palace keeps its own palace semantics, stem/branch, auxiliary/malefic/misc stars, local modifiers and other admitted local state. Borrow supplies a main-tone view; it does not replace the target.

## Relation role and dedupe

- `BORROWED_VIEW` is a conditional main-tone role, not an ordinary `OPPOSITE` vote.
- The same physical source/main-tone semantic cannot be counted again as ordinary opposite evidence for the same target and semantic class.
- Independently admissible opposite or sanhe evidence remains in its proper relation role.
- No numeric precedence, vote count, or “borrow is X% strength” is authorized.

## Topic boundary

Topic changes relevance and interpretation, not physical borrowing rules. Historical non-opposite or topic-special borrow claims remain `RAW_CANDIDATE` under Named Pattern/Exception and are OFF by default.

## Prohibited inferences

- empty → bad/weak/no event;
- borrowed star physically moved to target;
- auxiliary/malefic relocation;
- new transform or new brightness minted at target;
- target-local facts erased;
- borrowed and opposite views counted as two votes;
- projected/borrowed evidence re-projected;
- a topic example generalized into new borrow physics.
